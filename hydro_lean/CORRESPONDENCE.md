# Correspondence — from a denotational contract to a theorem about the machine

How `paxos_core.ensures` (a fact at `Values`) becomes `paxos_safe_sched'` (a fact about
every run of the concurrent step machine), what that theorem quantifies over, what is
deliberately *not* proven, and what a reviewer must judge by reading. Names are the
anchors; line numbers drift. History by D-number in `FINDINGS.md`.

## The claim, in one paragraph

Programs are written once against `HydroSem` (`Sem.lean`) and interpreted many ways.
**`Values`** (`Values.lean`) is the specification: cluster streams are graded quotient
pools and every nondeterministic choice is explicit *decision data*. **`SchedSem`**
(`Sched.lean`) is the concurrent machine: plain lists on a global step clock,
per-pair delivery cursors, per-member tick pacing, no quotients anywhere. The
correspondence says: **every machine run, under every schedule, at every finite
horizon, is covered by a denotational run at decisions derived from the schedule** —
and at every observation the program actually makes (tick batches, snapshots) the
coverage is an equality. The payoff is `paxos_safe_sched'` (`Paxos/CoupleWf.lean`):
per-slot agreement of Paxos commits holds for the *step machine* at every pacing,
schedule and horizon, **premise-free** — no satisfiability hypothesis, no decision
argument — obtained by transporting the denotational contract along the coupling.

```
      Values  ──── MonoRel (monotonicity for free)
        ▲     ──── Eager   (data ⊗ denotation: the executables)
        │
   CoupleSem    the corner: each operator DERIVES its Values decision from its
        │       machine leg and carries the coupling proof in its carrier
        ▼
     SchedSem   (lists, steps, cursors, pacing)
```

Five instances of one signature. `CoupleSem` is the coupling instance: its carriers
pair a `SchedSem` run with a `Values` run and the relation between them, so
instantiating a program at it **is** running the simulation argument over the program's
own syntax, operator by operator.

## Rosetta: what a distributed-systems reader expects → what is here

| you expect | here |
|---|---|
| a simulation relation between concrete and abstract states | `ListLe ord ret` (`Transfer.lean`): the grade-indexed concrete-list-to-quotient relation (prefix at TotalOrder, sub-multiset at NoOrder, destuttered/support at AtLeastOnce) — on **wires**, not on a global state |
| a refinement mapping | the derived decisions (`batchDerive`, `snapDerive`, …): the abstract nondeterminism *computed from the schedule*; `corr_batch`: exact at its own horizon |
| the inductive invariant over executions | none per program: the per-operator coupling proofs in `Couple.lean` + the per-module naming lemmas generated by `hydro def` (`<M>_co_sr/_rr/_wf`) |
| the protocol argument (quorum intersection, ballot provenance) | `paxos_core`'s `slot_functional` prove leg and the K4 `invariant` clause on its `a_log` knot (`Paxos/PaxosCore.lean`) — at `Values`, never machine-side |
| the adversary | a `(pacing, sched, dec)` tuple: per-member tick skeletons, per-pair cursors, timing verdicts (`SCHED_AUDIT.md`) |
| non-vacuity | the premises are inhabited by construction (the coupling is built, not postulated); `lake exe paxos` and `#guard`s run the scenarios; `lake exe falsify` shows the guards bite |
| liveness | `LIVENESS.md`: decision chains; `cq_live_chain`, `collect_quorum_tight` |
| adequacy / backward simulation | deliberately absent (below) |

## How the proof is layered

**1. `Transfer.lean` — per-operator coupling content.** `ListLe`, its preservation
through every machine construction, the derived decisions, and read agreement
(`sched_snap_eq`: truncation *is* the decision at consumption sites, so the `Values`
side expresses exactly what the machine consumed).

**2. `Couple.lean` — the corner.** Each carrier packs four things:

```lean
sr  : Fin n → StepHist α             -- the machine leg, SchedSem verbatim
rr  : Fin n → PoolCarrier α ord ret  -- a PLAIN Values leg
wf  : Prop                           -- residual well-formedness
cpl : wf → ∀ i, ListLe ord ret ((sr i).view Tc) (rr i)
```

at two ambient horizons (couple-at-`Tc`, derive-at-`Td`, `Tc ≤ Td`). Every
decision-mediated op derives its own `Values` decision from its machine leg (`batch`'s
abstract leg is `(Values).batch s.rr (batchDerive (pacing ℓ) Td s.sr)`), so its `cpl` is
its realization theorem at its own derived decision — no decision environment, no
satisfiability side condition (D39). In-tick operators carry the List-vs-quotient
coupling per operator (`CoBounded`/`CoBSing`, D60); a `tick` block's `wf` is its body's
coupling (`CoTick.scanWf`), under which the machine former coerced is the denotation
former on coerced inputs (`CoTick.scan_comm`). Knots close by induction on the horizon
through the machine fixpoint equation (`co_fix_cpl`, `co_tick_fix_cpl`); what remains
per knot is the **`wf` triple** — the body is causal, its `Values` leg is a Kleene
ascent, the coupling survives one body step — discharged with the instance-generic knot
bodies of `HydroSem.fix` (D38) and the kit in `SchedCausal.lean`/`KnotTactics.lean`/
`WfTactics.lean`, all generated per knot by `hydro def` (`<M>.<w>_co_wf₁`).

**3. `CoupleProj.lean` — the glue.** For every operator, once-proven `rfl` lemmas
identify the corner's projections with the `Values`/`SchedSem` op; `co_transfer [defs]`
pushes `.rr`/`.sr` through a program op by op. Kernel defeq through `k` nested knots is
exponential in `k` (D40), so the naming identities are **per module** and assembled
with modules folded: *the per-program content of a machine-run safety theorem is its
module naming lemmas plus one contract application.*

## Walkthrough A: `cq_safe_sched'` (`Std/Quorum.lean`) — the smallest complete pipeline

Every key `k` the machine's `collect_quorum` has emitted by step `T` holds `mn` `Ok`
votes among the consumed pool of a derived decision. Four moves:

```lean
  -- (1) the refinement mapping, computed: the ∃-witness is the batch decision this
  --     schedule realized (collect_quorum_vdec = batchDerive over the machine input)
  refine ⟨collect_quorum_vdec (Td := T) (pacing := pacing) () mn mx h, ?_⟩
  -- (2) the simulation: instantiate at the corner and read off .cpl; the wf residue
  --     is the generated collect_quorum_co_wf₁
  have hcpl := (collect_quorum (CoupleSem … T T _) () (CoStream.inputC h v hc) mn mx
      (CoDec.batch _)).1.cpl …
  -- (0) name the legs: the corner's sr IS collect_quorum@SchedSem, its rr IS
  --     collect_quorum@Values at the derived decision (collect_quorum_co_sr₁/_rr₁)
  -- (3) the abstract contract: collect_quorum.ensures at Values — emit_sound
  -- (4) transport the machine's emission along the sub-multiset coupling
  exact hens.emit_sound i k (Multiset.mem_of_le hcpl (Multiset.mem_coe.mpr hk))
```

Absent: any induction over executions, any invariant about the block's internal state.
The induction happened once per operator inside `CoupleSem`; `collect_quorum`'s
protocol reasoning lives in its own contract on the `Values` side.

## Walkthrough B: `paxos_safe_sched'` (`Paxos/CoupleWf.lean`) — the headline

```lean
theorem paxos_safe_sched' (hnA : mem acc ≤ 2 * f + 1)
    -- section variables: cpS/cpV/hcp, ckS/ckV/hck — client-payload and checkpoint
    --   inputs, machine + abstract, coupled; sdec/ssched — ANY schedule; T — ANY
    --   horizon; pacing — ANY per-member tick pacing
    (i j : Fin (mem prop)) (s : Nat) (w w' : Option P)
    (hw  : (s, w)  ∈ ((paxos_core (SchedSem L mem pacing) .guarded … ).2 i).view T)
    (hw' : (s, w') ∈ ((paxos_core (SchedSem L mem pacing) .guarded … ).2 j).view T) :
    w = w'
```

No decision argument, no satisfiability hypothesis. The body is the same four moves
with four names: `paxos_co_wf` (the five knots' `wf` triples, built from the generated
`co_wf` artifacts) → `paxos_co_cpl` (`= .cpl paxos_co_wf`) → `paxos_co_sr`/`paxos_co_rr`
(the legs named; `Paxos/CoupleSafety.lean`) → `paxos_core.ensures … .slot_functional`
at the derived record `paxosVDecG`. Where the intuitions land:

- **Quorum intersection and provenance** are in `paxos_core`'s prove leg and the K4
  `invariant` on the `a_log` knot (over log entries; `nodup_inter_of_length` is the
  pigeonhole) — `Values` only. This file transports.
- **The invariant over executions** is the per-operator coupling plus the naming
  identities; no Paxos-specific simulation reasoning exists.
- **Per knot**: causality (the head-dispatch walker over `SchedCausal.lean`), Kleene
  ascent (one `MonoRel`/`MonoHRel` instantiation), one coupling step (the `∀ H'`-generic
  body re-instantiated at a horizon-lowered corner).

## The refinement mapping, computed: `batchDerive`

The machine side of a batched input is a `StepHist` on the step clock plus a per-member
pacing; the `Values` side is the member's pool plus a `BatchCuts` decision (a list of
per-tick multisets). `batchDerive pacing Td h` reads the cuts off the run: the items that
arrived between consecutive ticks of this member below `Td`. `corr_batch` (realization):
`batchCuts v 0 (batchDerive … T) = batchDerive … T` — the derived decision is *legal* and
*exact* at its own horizon, so the `Values` batch at the derived decision is the
machine's tick sequence coerced, not merely above it. The decision space was built as
the denotational shadow of the schedule space; derivation is the shadow map. It is
horizon-monotone, which is what makes decision *chains* the shadow of infinite behaviors
(`LIVENESS.md`).

Oblivious schedules cover adaptive adversaries for safety: cursors, pacing and verdicts
are functions of the step only, and the machine is deterministic given the tuple, so
any adaptive adversary's realized run *is* some tuple (`SCHED_AUDIT.md` S4 §12).

## Unbounded commutative folds: a decision is a chain, not a set of draws

A fold over an unordered unbounded stream (`fold` at NoOrder) is read through snapshots;
the `Values` model of its decision is a `SnapshotCuts` *chain* — successive per-tick
increments of the consumed multiset — not an arbitrary family of subsets. The machine
gets consistency for free from having one history; the derived chain (`snapDerive`) is
exactly its consumption sequence, and the fold's `FoldOk` obligation (commutativity at
NoOrder) is what makes the quotient-side fold well defined on the chain. Reordering
inside a cut is paid once, by the fold's obligation, never by the coupling.

## What is deliberately NOT proven

- **Adequacy / bisimulation.** There is no `∀ decisions ∃ schedule` theorem, and there
  cannot be: `flattenUnordered` publishes a list whose order the *type* forgets but the
  machine determines, so a downstream `assume_ordering` realizes one order per schedule
  while the abstract `OrderSelection` space licenses every permutation. The quotient
  over-approximates on purpose: safety transfer needs machine ⊆ denotation only, and
  the surplus makes program obligations robust to weaker transports. This is a forward
  simulation, by design.
- **Real Rust execution.** The machine is a semantics of the Hydro combinators, not of
  compiled Rust; the 1:1 program-text mirror (`DOCTRINE.md` P1) is the bridge
  discipline, and `SCHED_AUDIT.md` is the fidelity audit of the machine against the
  runtime.
- **Liveness** is proven only where `LIVENESS.md` says so (`cq_live_chain`); Paxos-scale
  liveness is queued.

## Trust base

Every headline is audited by `#print axioms` in `AxCheck.lean`: `paxos_safe_sched'`,
`cq_safe_sched'`, and the stack they consume (`paxos_co_sr`, `paxos_co_rr`,
`paxos_co_wf`, `paxos_co_cpl`, the generated `co_*` classes). Expected foundation:
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; no `sorryAx`, no custom axioms anywhere in
`Hydro/`. The quotient carriers (`Grades.lean`) rest on core `Quotient`, i.e. on
`Quot.sound` — the same story as Mathlib's.

Axioms are half of a trust base. The other half is the **definitions in the theorem
statements** — above all `SchedSem`, because `paxos_safe_sched'` says "safe in every
execution *of this machine*". A machine that secretly could not reorder messages,
silently synchronized ticks, or dropped buffers would make the theorem true and
worthless. `#print axioms` cannot catch that; the next section is the review that can.

## Auditing the trust boundary: the `Sched` machine

`Sched.lean` is deliberately small and the `SchedSem` instance is one page. Judge each
definition by what would go wrong if it were subtly different; `TransferChecks.lean`
machine-checks a positive instance of each adversarial behavior (a `#guard` proves the
model *admits* a behavior; no `#guard` can prove it admits *enough* — maximality of the
adversary is what this review is for). The systematic audit against the Rust runtime is
`SCHED_AUDIT.md`.

- **Wires** (`StepHist`): histories that can only grow — `view t` is a prefix of
  `view (t+1)` by type. A sub-multiset relation here would let the machine forget.
- **Delivery** (`deliver`, `cumMax`): a per-pair cursor `Nat → Nat`, monotonized; reads
  the sender's `view (t-1)` (the `+1` network floor). Any cursor function is legal:
  unbounded delay, silence forever, stall-at-`k` (crash), per-recipient divergence of a
  broadcast. Nothing schedules on message content — cursors and pacing are functions of
  the step only, by type.
- **Fan-in** (`values`/`union`): takes no decision; the interleaving *emerges* from
  delivery timing, with a canonical within-step tiebreak (`SCHED_AUDIT.md` F4).
- **Ticks and consumption** (`pacing`, `batchesFrom`): per-member skeletons, arbitrary
  (stutter ticks observable where they should be); a tick consumes everything available
  — "partial batches" arise only from arrival timing.
- **Re-monotonization** (`famFreeze`): a no-op for op-built flows (F3 keeps the
  transparency theorem on the follow-up list); it cannot invent states.
- **Emission**: a `tick` block computes each tick's emissions on the machine's plain
  lists; the corner couples them to the quotient per operator (D61). No linearization
  decision exists.
- **The knot** (`fix_stream`/`fix_tick`): the Kleene diagonal with a one-step `shift` —
  cycle unfolding *is* step progression; the `+1` floor excludes exactly the Zeno
  executions under which the diagonal is ill-defined, and nothing else.

### The audit checklist

| judge by reading | definition | existence witness (`TransferChecks.lean`) |
|---|---|---|
| histories are immutable (prefix, not sub-multiset) | `StepHist.mono` | — (type-level) |
| cursors express unbounded delay & silence | `deliver`/`cumMax`: any `Nat → Nat` | §2 stalled cursor |
| cross-sender reordering is schedule-reachable | fan-in takes no decision | §1 two schedules, equal multisets, different lists |
| per-pair FIFO, no in-flight corruption/duplication | `deliver` is `take` of the sent list | §3 delivered views are prefixes of sent |
| nothing schedules on message content | cursor/pacing **types** | — (type-level) |
| duplicates born at sampling, not transport | `sample_every`; `deliver` can only `take` | §5 consecutive stutter, destuttered by the coupling |
| ticks unsynchronized across locations **and members**; stutter ticks observable | `pacing` per member; `batchesFrom` | §4, §9 per-member skew |
| consume-all-at-tick | `batchesFrom` drops exactly the consumed prefix | §4 flattened batches equal across pacings |
| the knot pays a step but only a step | `shift` + Kleene diagonal | §6 offspring lands cause+1; overtaking still free |
| re-monotonization cannot fabricate states | `famFreeze_eq_raw` | — (theorem) |
| unordered emissions take no decision | the `tick` block on plain lists + the corner's per-op coupling | — (definition) |
| stabilization hypotheses are inhabited | `StabilizesAt` kit (`TransferTheory.lean`) | §8 |
| **the model is adversarial *enough*** | — | **not machine-checkable; this checklist is the review** |

One modeling commitment to weigh explicitly: per-pair FIFO transport (fail-stop TCP —
no selective drop, no in-flight duplication; `SCHED_AUDIT.md` F6). It errs in the
direction visible to review, and it is not hidden behind a quotient.

## A cautionary section: the D37 vacuity

The *first* machine-safety headline was `∀ d`-quantified over a decision environment
with a satisfiability premise `hsat : δ T d`. Attempting to *discharge* that premise —
to show the theorem non-vacuous — revealed it was **unsatisfiable for nested knots**: a
bridge condition inside the knot's δ forced two consecutive Kleene stages equal, so the
headline was vacuously true at every horizon ≥ 1 in a zero-sorry, gate-green tree. The
δ design was repaired, then dissolved by the corner, whose coupling is constructed, not
postulated (D37–D42). The lesson stands and is now a rule (`DOCTRINE.md` R7): **a premise
you have never inhabited is a hole exactly the size of your theorem** — every
quantified side condition on trusted-statement machinery ships with the witness that
inhabits it.
